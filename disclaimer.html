Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: June 2026

⚕️ QuitCoach AI is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency services immediately.

1. Not a medical device

QuitCoach AI is a behavioural coaching and support application designed to assist people who wish to quit smoking or vaping. It is not a medical device, is not regulated as a medical device, and has not been evaluated or approved by any medical regulatory authority.

The app does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, including nicotine dependence.

2. Not a substitute for professional medical advice

Nothing in QuitCoach AI — including the AI coaching sessions, daily lessons, withdrawal information, symptom tracking, or any other content — constitutes medical advice. This information is provided for general educational and coaching purposes only.

Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare professional with any questions you may have regarding:

  • Smoking cessation treatment options
  • Nicotine replacement therapy (NRT) dosing and protocols
  • Prescription cessation medications such as varenicline (Champix/Chantix) or bupropion
  • Withdrawal symptoms that are severe, prolonged, or concerning
  • Any pre-existing medical conditions that may be affected by quitting smoking

3. The AI coach is not a therapist or doctor

The AI coaching functionality in QuitCoach AI is powered by a large language model (Claude, by Anthropic). While the coach is designed to provide evidence-based cessation support, it is an AI system — not a licensed therapist, psychologist, physician, or healthcare professional.

The AI coach cannot assess your individual medical situation, and its responses should not be treated as personalised medical recommendations.

4. Evidence basis

The techniques used in QuitCoach AI are drawn from published, peer-reviewed research in smoking cessation, including:

  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) principles for addiction
  • Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) techniques
  • Marlatt and Gordon's Relapse Prevention Model
  • Clinical NRT step-down protocols
  • Mindfulness-based craving management (urge surfing)
  • Motivational interviewing principles

That the techniques are evidence-based does not mean that QuitCoach AI itself has been clinically tested or validated as a treatment. The app applies these techniques in a coaching context.

5. Withdrawal symptoms

The withdrawal symptom information in QuitCoach AI is based on published clinical data about nicotine withdrawal timelines. Individual experiences vary significantly. If you experience withdrawal symptoms that are severe, unusual, or that you are concerned about, please consult a healthcare professional.

Seek immediate medical attention if you experience chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, or any symptoms that feel like a medical emergency.

6. Outcomes

Quitting smoking is difficult and outcomes vary significantly between individuals. QuitCoach AI does not guarantee that you will successfully quit smoking. Success depends on many factors including your level of dependence, personal motivation, support network, and individual circumstances.

7. Mental health

Nicotine withdrawal can affect mood, anxiety levels, and mental health. If you experience significant depression, anxiety, or other mental health concerns during your quit attempt, please reach out to a mental health professional. QuitCoach AI is not equipped to provide mental health crisis support.

If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately.
